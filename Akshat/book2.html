The Boy in the Striped Pajamas

Summary

Author: John Boyne
Published in 2006, The Boy in the Striped Pajamas is a New York Times No.1 Bestseller that also has a live action film based on it. The book is set in a pre World War 2 time. It tells the story of 2 young boys. One who was a prisoner in the Auschwitz concentration camp and another, who was the son of the officer who recently was stationed near the camp, having left behind a happy and peaceful life in Berlin.
