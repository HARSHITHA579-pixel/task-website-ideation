Three Men on a Boat

Summary

Author: Jerone K Jerome
Published in 1889, this classic was originally intended to be a serious travel guide, with accounts of local history about the region. Three London gentlemen (not forgetting their pet dog) take a vacation rowing down the Thames, encountering various mishaps and misadventures along the way.
