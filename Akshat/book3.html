The Complete Harry Potter Collection

Summary

Author: J.K. Rowling
Published between 1997 and 2007, the Harry Potter collection is a classic that is beloved by many. Orphan Harry learns he is a wizard on his 11th birthday when Hagrid escorts him to magic-teaching Hogwarts School. As a baby, his mother's love protected him and vanquished the villain Voldemort, leaving the child famous as "The Boy who Lived." With his friends Hermione and Ron, Harry has to defeat the returned "He Who Must Not Be Named."
