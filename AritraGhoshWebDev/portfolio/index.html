About Me

Introduction

Hi, I'm Aritra. Born in 2005. I'm from West Bengal, India. Currently, I'm in Vellore, Tamil Nadu for my bachelors degree. At my core, I'm someone who deeply values connection and community. The people in my life mean the world to me — especially my mother, who is my absolute best friend and biggest support system. I love tackling life alongside a close-knit group of friends, whether we're collaborating on a creative project or just hanging out. I've always got a curious mind, so in my free time, you'll usually find me learning Spanish, keeping up with the stock market, or unwinding by managing Minecraft servers. There's always more to it.

My Current Pursuit

RoboVITics

As a Junior Core in Web Development at RoboVITics, I have the exciting opportunity to bridge the gap between cutting-edge hardware innovation and seamless digital experiences. Being part of such a premier technical community at VIT allows me to leverage my expertise in modern web stacks, including MERN, to build and maintain the platforms that showcase our engineering achievements. It is an incredible environment rooted in originality and perseverance, and I take pride in ensuring our club's online presence is as robust, dynamic, and forward-thinking as the robots we engineer.

Team Orcus

As a Junior Core in the Electrical Department for Team Orcus, my primary focus is on the intricate hardware and power systems that bring our combat robots to life. My role involves hands-on circuit design, component integration, and embedded systems programming. From drafting precise schematics and component models in Autodesk Fusion 360 to ensuring reliable power distribution during intense arena combat, I am responsible for the electrical integrity and responsive control systems of our bots, translating complex hardware challenges into robust, battle-ready solutions.
