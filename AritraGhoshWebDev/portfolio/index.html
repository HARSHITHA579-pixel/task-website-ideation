About Me

Introduction

Hi, I'm Aritra. Born in 2005. I'm from West Bengal, India. Currently, I'm in Vellore, Tamil Nadu for my bachelors degree. At my core, I'm someone who deeply values connection and community. The people in my life mean the world to me — especially my mother, who is my absolute best friend and biggest support system. I love tackling life alongside a close-knit group of friends, whether we're collaborating on a creative project or just hanging out. I've always got a curious mind, so in my free time, you'll usually find me learning Spanish, keeping up with the stock market, or unwinding by managing Minecraft servers. There's always more to it.
